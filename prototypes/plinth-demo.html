Example briefing: your portfolio is down 15% this month

What you'd get before anything happens
What happened. US stocks fell 24% over three weeks after a surprise rate rise. International stocks fell 20%. Your bonds rose 2%, so your portfolio is down 15%, from $24,870 to $21,090.
How it compares. Falls of 15% or more have happened many times in US stock market history. Every one so far has recovered, some within months and some only after several years. Nobody knows which kind this is.
What it means for you. You told me you need this money in more than 10 years. Your House fund and emergency fund are in cash, so this fall doesn't touch your house plans.
Your options. Nothing happens until you pick one.
If it were up to the agent: rebalance. You said you won't need this money for more than 10 years, and rebalancing is what your plan was set up to do. It's your call.
This is an example with made-up market moves. The agent takes no action until you choose.

What the agent can and can't do

No person or AI can reliably predict where markets go next, and agents that trade often tend to lose money to fees and taxes. Your agent's job is narrower: keep you diversified, keep costs low, rebalance on schedule, cut your tax bill, and stop panic-selling when markets drop. Past returns don't guarantee future ones.
